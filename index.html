Hi!, welcome to...
TGRAFIX Design Laboratory

I'am  Joseph Tuquib, a Graphic & Web Designer and Computer Trainer based in the Philippines.

For the past decade, I’ve been happily obsessed with turning ideas into cool, functional digital stuff. Whether it’s designing responsive websites, crafting clean UI, creating animations, working on print media, or teaching others how to master new tech, I love mixing creativity with practical design.

My main goal? To help brands look awesome, tell their story, and actually make an impact—both online and off (and save the world from bad typography along the way!).

I’m currently freelancing and always down to team up on new projects. If you’re looking for someone who brings top-tier creativity, solid attention to detail, and won't ghost your messages, let’s chat!
